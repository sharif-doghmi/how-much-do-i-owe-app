# Privacy Policy

**How Much Do I Owe?** app does not collect, transmit, or share any user data, and does not have the functionality to do so.

**How Much Do I Owe?** is a basic static app. It is client-side only. It doesn't connect to a backend server or the internet. It does not initiate or receive any network connections.

If you have any questions or comments, please contact how.much.do.i.owe.app@gmail.com
